Synchronous mode — Rules

- Guesses must be five-letter words in the server's Wordle allow-list.
- 5 total guesses per player.
- The host selects a 1-, 3-, or 5-minute limit for every round before the match starts (default: 1 minute).
- Guesses are secretly registered — no one sees others' guesses until the timer ends or everyone has submitted.
- When the round closes, all guesses reveal simultaneously with standard color feedback.
- If exactly one player guesses the word, they win immediately.
- If two or more players guess the word in the same round, those players advance to a bboy dance-off (a timed tap/rhythm minigame) to decide the winner.
- If no one guesses correctly, play continues to the next guess (up to 5 rounds).
- If no one guesses the word within 5 tries, the game ends with no winner (or reveals the word, per host setting).

Real-time mode — Rules

- Guesses must be five-letter words in the server's Wordle allow-list.
- 10 total guesses per player.
- No shared timer or turn order — every player submits guesses as fast as they want.
- Guesses are visible in real time (or per house rule) as players submit them.
- The first player to guess the word correctly wins instantly and ends the game for everyone.
- Players who exhaust all 10 guesses without success are eliminated from contention but the race continues for others.
- If every player exhausts 10 guesses with no winner, the game ends with no winner (or reveals the word, per host setting).
